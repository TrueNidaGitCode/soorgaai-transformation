# Automotive Domain — AI Maturity Intelligence Brief

## Industry Overview
The automotive industry is undergoing its most significant transformation in over a century, driven by the convergence of electrification, software-defined vehicles (SDV), and artificial intelligence. OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers face simultaneous pressure to reduce time-to-market, improve quality, and deliver differentiated digital experiences.

## AI Opportunity Landscape

### Product Engineering
- ADAS and autonomous driving systems (perception, path planning, sensor fusion)
- Predictive quality and defect detection in manufacturing
- Generative AI for design simulation and materials optimization

### Manufacturing
- Predictive maintenance on production lines
- Computer vision for quality inspection
- Supply chain demand forecasting and disruption detection

### Aftersales & Customer Experience
- AI-powered diagnostics and remote vehicle health monitoring
- Personalized in-vehicle assistant and infotainment
- Dealer and service network optimization

## Key AI Maturity Benchmarks (Automotive)
| Dimension      | Stage 1 (Scramble) | Stage 3 (Alignment) | Stage 5 (AI-Fueled) |
|----------------|--------------------|---------------------|----------------------|
| AI Strategy    | None               | ADAS roadmap exists | AI-first product strategy |
