The checks are chosen with you, from the systems you connect.

How it works

  1. 01
    Connect — your booking or clinic system, CRM, phone and WhatsApp.
  2. 02
    Detect — checks run on your own records, daily or hourly.
  3. 03
    Explain — for each customer: what was found, why it matters, the next step.
  4. 04
    Act and measure — your team acts; SvargAI checks whether it worked.

SvargAI never contacts a customer. Your team decides.

Not another system to run

Your booking system records what happened.
SvargAI looks for who needs attention.

It stays your system of record. SvargAI does not replace it or ask your team to keep another one up to date.

A practical starting point

Connect one or two systems you already use. Run a small set of checks on your own records for thirty days, and review the customers SvargAI raises with your team before going further.
