Model Catalog

The evidence Arth recommends from, and the bands that decide what it may choose. Two benchmark tables are maintained: Strategy & Ops and Engineering. Scores and costs are seeded from the published comparisons and changed there — scripts/seed_strategy_ops_models.mjs and scripts/seed_engineering_models.mjs — so a number on this page always matches a table someone can check it against.

Confidence bands

Each table is split into thirds, measured from the scores it actually carries. Cob’s use case decides which band the work needs — judgement-led work takes Very High, extraction and classification take Medium — and Arth returns the cheapest models inside that band. The split is relative on purpose: a fixed cut would call every Strategy & Ops model medium and most Engineering models high, which is an artefact of two benchmarks being scaled differently.
