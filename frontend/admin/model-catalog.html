Model Catalog

The evidence Arth recommends from, and the range that decides what it may choose. Two benchmark tables are maintained: Strategy & Ops and Engineering. Scores and costs are seeded from the published comparisons and changed there — scripts/seed_strategy_ops_models.mjs and scripts/seed_engineering_models.mjs — so a number on this page always matches a table someone can check it against.

Acceptable range

The score band you are willing to ship. Arth returns the five cheapest models inside it, rather than the highest scoring one — which is the whole point of setting a range. Leave both blank to rank on balance instead.
