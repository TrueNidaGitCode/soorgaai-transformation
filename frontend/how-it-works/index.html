How it works

From the systems you already have to your own retention app.

Five steps, each handled by one part of Svarg. Your team describes the problem; Svarg plans, connects, builds and fits the app around how you already work.

  1. 01CobPlan
  2. 02AriaRun
  3. 03ArthConnect
  4. 04EameBuild
  5. 05YusuFit in

01CobPlanBuilt by Svarg

It starts from how your team works, not a template.

Describe your business and what you find out too late, in your own words. Cob works out which customer signals matter, where they live, and which risk to watch first, with the reasoning written out.

Cob is live today. Try it on your own business in a few minutes, no call needed.

Try Cob →
  1. 01

    Which signals matter

    The signs of a customer drifting away in your business, named in your own words: missed visits, longer gaps, unanswered messages, failed payments.

  2. 02

    Where to start, and why

    Every risk is weighed by what it costs you and how quickly it can be watched. One is recommended first, with the reason attached.

  3. 03

    What it will need

    Each signal carries what it depends on: the data, where it usually lives, and what is missing today. That is what Arth picks up.

02AriaRunPartner-powered

The right model for the job, already running.

Your app needs a model to read signals and explain each risk. Aria chooses one for what your app does and the constraints you set, and runs it for you. There is nothing to buy and no keys to manage.

Aria is delivered with specialist model and compute partners; Svarg makes sure the right one is in place for your app.

  1. 01

    Chosen for the work

    The model is matched to the job your app does and to what matters to you, such as privacy, cost or language. Sarvam’s models, built in India, are an option.

  2. 02

    Nothing to set up

    Hosting and compute are provided. Your application never holds an AI provider key, so there is none to leak.

  3. 03

    A ceiling on spend

    Every application has its own allowance, so a runaway loop cannot run up a bill. You can see how much each one has used on your Account page.

03ArthConnectPartner-powered

Your data stays where it is. Arth connects to it.

The signals already sit in your CRM, inbox, phone system and spreadsheets. Arth connects to them where they are and reads only what your app needs. There is nothing to migrate.

Arth is delivered with specialist data partners; Svarg makes sure your data arrives where your app needs it.

  1. 01

    Connect where it lives

    Your CRM (such as Zoho or LeadSquared), your phone system, WhatsApp, a database, or a simple upload.

  2. 02

    Only what the job needs

    Your app reads the fields each signal needs. Customer names, emails and phone numbers are replaced with stand-ins before any prompt reaches the AI, and every prompt is in a log you can audit.

  3. 03

    Gaps shown, not guessed

    Each source is matched against the signals Cob asked for, and anything missing is shown plainly, so you know what the app can and cannot see.

04EameBuildBuilt by Svarg

Your own retention app, with its AI agents.

Eame builds the application itself: your own app, at its own address, with AI agents that each watch for one kind of risk and show the records behind every alert.

  1. 01

    Agents for your risks

    Gone Quiet, Renewal Due, Unanswered Enquiry, Repeat Complaint and more, chosen for the risks Cob found in your business.

  2. 02

    Evidence on every alert

    Each alert links to the records it was found in, so your team knows who to call and why. No black box.

  3. 03

    It grows with you

    When your team needs something new, it is added to the same app. There is no new tool to adopt.

05YusuFit inBuilt by Svarg

It works where your team already works.

An app nobody opens is worth nothing. Yusu puts alerts and next steps into the tools your team already uses, so acting on a risk is part of the day, not another tab.

Yusu is under active development. This is the direction, not a finished product.

  1. 01

    Inside your tools

    Alerts and follow-ups reach your team in the tools they already have open, instead of a separate portal.

  2. 02

    One team first

    Rolled out to one team, proven, then widened. The same careful start every Svarg app gets.

  3. 03

    Changes as you do

    As your team changes how it works, the app adapts with it, with no tickets to file and no waiting on us.
