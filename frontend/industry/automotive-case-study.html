Case Study — Automotive

Sound familiar?

This is the exact kind of scenario Cob was built for — not hypothetical, but the day-to-day reality many automotive engineering teams recognize immediately.

Before Cob

  • Defects, incidents, and support tickets arrive from multiple sources — email, tickets, calls, dashboards.
  • Triage happens manually, team by team, with no shared view.
  • Prioritization depends on who escalates loudest, not on actual business impact.
  • Recurring root causes go unnoticed until they've already cost weeks of rework.
→

What Cob generates

  • A blueprint that classifies and routes defects, incidents, and tickets automatically.
  • Cross-team visibility into what's actually breaking, and where.
  • Prioritization grounded in business impact, not urgency of complaint.
  • Root-cause patterns surfaced before they become a trend.

This is one scenario. Yours will look different — but Cob starts the same way: understanding what you're actually dealing with.
