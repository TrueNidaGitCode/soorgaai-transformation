Aria — Data Readiness

Your data isn't missing.
It's just not safe to use yet.

Regulated, sensitive, or customer-linked data often can't be handed to an AI team directly — not because it lacks value, but because using it raises real privacy and compliance risk. Aria is how Svarg addresses that gap: making an enterprise's own data usable for AI development without exposing anything it shouldn't.

Talk to us about your data

How it works

Three ways Aria prepares your data

All processing happens inside your own environment — on-prem, VPC, or your own cluster. Nothing sensitive ever needs to leave.

01

Redaction & de-identification

Sensitive and personally identifiable information is detected and removed before any dataset reaches an AI workflow.

02

Synthetic data generation

Realistic, representative datasets are generated from your own patterns — without a single real record leaving your environment.

03

Edge-case coverage

Rare scenarios and anomalies are generated deliberately, so AI systems are tested against situations real data rarely provides enough examples of.

How this fits together

Built by specialists. Delivered through Svarg.

Svarg doesn't build every layer of the AI stack ourselves — and we don't think we should. Aria is delivered in partnership with specialized data infrastructure providers who focus on exactly this problem, every day. Our role is to make sure the right capability shows up at the right point in your blueprint, integrated with the rest of your AI strategy — not to reinvent expertise that already exists.
