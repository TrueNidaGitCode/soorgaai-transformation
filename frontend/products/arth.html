Arth — Technology & Infrastructure

The right compute. The right model.
Not whatever's convenient.

Most enterprises default to whatever GPU capacity and general-purpose model they already have access to — not because it's the right fit, but because procuring anything else takes months and a large budget. For specialized, safety-critical, or regulated workloads, that default often isn't good enough. Arth is how SoorgaAI addresses that gap: matching each workload to infrastructure and models actually built for it.

How it works

Three ways Arth fits infrastructure to the workload

Compute and models are matched to what the workload actually needs — not defaulted to whatever's already on hand.

01

Compute, provisioned fast

GPU capacity is deployed in days, not the months typical of traditional procurement — with the flexibility to place workloads where data residency and compliance require.

02

Domain-specific models where they matter

General-purpose LLMs aren't always enough for specialized or safety-critical contexts. Purpose-built models — trained on the standards and constraints of a specific domain — are used where they outperform generic ones.

03

Optimization built in

Cluster configuration and model optimization are handled as part of deployment, not left as a separate multi-month project after the fact.

How this fits together

Built by specialists. Delivered through SoorgaAI.

SoorgaAI doesn't operate GPU infrastructure or train domain-specific models ourselves — and we don't think we should. Arth is delivered in partnership with specialized compute and model providers who focus on exactly this problem, every day. Our role is to make sure the right capability shows up at the right point in your blueprint, integrated with the rest of your AI strategy — not to reinvent expertise that already exists.
