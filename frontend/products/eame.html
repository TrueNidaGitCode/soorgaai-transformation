Eame — Skills & Workforce

A strategy doesn't execute itself.
A team does.

Buying an AI tool and knowing how to get value from it are two different problems. Teams need practical, hands-on capability with the specific tools and workflows a blueprint calls for — and sometimes the right skills simply aren't in the building yet, with a hiring cycle an AI initiative doesn't have time to wait for. Eame is how SoorgaAI addresses that gap: building capability inside your team, or bringing it in fast when you can't wait to hire.

How it works

Three ways Eame builds capability

Focused on getting your team applying new capability to real work — not just aware that the tools exist.

01

Training built around your actual tools

Not generic AI literacy — hands-on capability with the specific tools and workflows your blueprint calls for, so training translates directly into day-to-day work.

02

Fast augmentation when hiring can't keep up

When a skills gap can't wait for a traditional hiring cycle, vetted specialists are placed quickly to keep an initiative moving.

03

Hands-on delivery support

Beyond training, support applying new capability to real implementation work — so it sticks, rather than staying theoretical.

How this fits together

Built by specialists. Delivered through SoorgaAI.

SoorgaAI doesn't run training programs or staff augmentation ourselves — and we don't think we should. Eame is delivered in partnership with specialized workforce development providers who focus on exactly this problem, every day. Our role is to make sure the right capability shows up at the right point in your blueprint, integrated with the rest of your AI strategy — not to reinvent expertise that already exists.
