# AI Maturity Stages

## Purpose

This document defines the AI maturity stages used by the Inceptly Assessment Engine.

The assessment engine uses these stages to
- evaluate organizational AI maturity
- generate maturity-aligned assessment questions
- calculate maturity scoring
- generate AI transformation recommendations
- identify transformation roadmap priorities

---

# Stage 1 — AI Scramble

## Definition

Organizations begin experimenting with AI.

AI efforts are isolated and exploratory.

## Characteristics

- Small pilots and proof-of-concepts
- Individual teams trying AI tools
- No clear AI strategy or governance

---

# Stage 2 — AI Pivot

## Definition

Organizations start taking AI seriously and investing in capabilities.

AI becomes a strategic priority, but adoption is still fragmented.

## Characteristics

- Increased budget for AI initiatives
- Adoption of AI tools for productivity
- Initial hiring or training in AI skills

---

# Stage 3 — AI Alignment

## Definition

AI initiatives become aligned with business goals.

AI moves from experimentation to structured implementation.

## Characteristics

- AI projects linked to business outcomes
- Cross-team collaboration begins
- Early governance and data strategies emerge

---

# Stage 4 — AI Transform

## Definition

AI begins transforming how the organization operates.

AI starts delivering enterprise-level impact.

## Characteristics

- AI integrated into core workflows
- AI-driven insights supporting decision-making
- Automation of key processes

---

# Stage 5 — AI-Fueled Enterprise

## Definition

AI becomes a core operating layer of the business.

AI is no longer a tool — it becomes a fundamental capability of the organization.

## Characteristics

- AI embedded across operations, products, and decision-making
- Intelligent automation across departments
- Continuous learning and innovation powered by AI
