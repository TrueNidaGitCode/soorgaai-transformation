# ADAS — Advanced Driver Assistance Systems

**Business Domain:** Automotive  
**Status:** Planned

---

## Domain Overview

ADAS encompasses the AI-driven perception, sensor fusion, decision-making, and
actuation systems that enable assisted and automated driving capabilities.

This is the highest-risk AI domain in automotive — ADAS AI systems are safety-critical
and subject to ISO 26262 and SOTIF requirements.

---

## Key AI Opportunities

- **Perception systems** — Object detection, classification, and tracking (camera, LiDAR, radar)
- **Sensor fusion** — Multi-modal data fusion for reliable environmental understanding
- **Scenario generation** — AI-generated test scenarios for edge case validation
- **Requirements generation** — AI-assisted system and software requirements from scenarios
- **Simulation** — AI-driven virtual validation environments
- **Regression detection** — Automated detection of ADAS performance regression in CI/CD

---

## Safety Criticality

ADAS AI systems are classified as high-risk under the EU AI Act and must comply with:
- ISO 26262 (Functional Safety)
- ISO 21448 (SOTIF — Safety of the Intended Functionality)
- UNECE WP.29 regulations for automated vehicles

All AI strategy decisions for ADAS must be reviewed by functional safety engineers.
