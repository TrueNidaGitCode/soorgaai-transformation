# Connectivity

**Business Domain:** Automotive  
**Status:** Planned

---

## Domain Overview

Vehicle connectivity encompasses telematics, over-the-air (OTA) update platforms,
connected services, V2X communication, and vehicle data ecosystems.

AI is central to connected vehicle services — from personalizing in-vehicle experiences
to optimizing OTA rollout strategies and detecting cyber threats.

---

## Key AI Opportunities

- **OTA optimization** — ML-driven campaign scheduling and rollout risk prediction
- **Connected services personalization** — Behaviour-based service recommendations
- **V2X data processing** — AI for vehicle-to-infrastructure and vehicle-to-vehicle data
- **Cybersecurity** — Anomaly detection on vehicle network traffic (intrusion detection)
- **Fleet analytics** — AI-driven insights from connected vehicle fleets
- **Digital twin** — Connected vehicle state modeling for predictive services

---

## Regulatory and Standards Context

- UNECE WP.29 / ISO 21434 (Cybersecurity)
- GDPR for connected vehicle data and driver profiles
- ISO 24089 (OTA updates)
