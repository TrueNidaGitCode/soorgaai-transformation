# Manufacturing

**Business Domain:** Automotive  
**Status:** Planned

---

## Domain Overview

Automotive manufacturing encompasses production line operations, quality control,
supply chain management, and factory automation.

AI is enabling smart manufacturing — from visual quality inspection to predictive
maintenance of production equipment and AI-driven supply chain optimization.

---

## Key AI Opportunities

- **Visual quality inspection** — Computer vision for defect detection on production lines
- **Predictive maintenance** — ML models for factory equipment health monitoring
- **Production optimization** — AI scheduling and throughput optimization
- **Supply chain AI** — Demand forecasting, supplier risk, and inventory optimization
- **Digital twin** — Factory-level simulation for process optimization
- **Energy optimization** — AI-driven energy consumption reduction in production

---

## Regulatory and Standards Context

- ISO 9001 / IATF 16949 (Automotive quality management)
- Industry 4.0 / Smart manufacturing standards
- Safety regulations for AI-driven automation (ISO 10218 for robots)
