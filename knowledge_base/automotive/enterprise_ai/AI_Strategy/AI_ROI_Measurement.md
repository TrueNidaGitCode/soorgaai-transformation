# AI ROI Measurement

**Domain:** AI Strategy  
**Category:** Business Value

---

## Purpose

Provides frameworks and approaches for measuring, tracking, and communicating the
return on investment of enterprise AI initiatives in automotive organizations.

---

## Why ROI Measurement Matters

Without clear ROI measurement:
- AI investment approval becomes political rather than evidence-based
- Successful initiatives are not scaled; underperforming initiatives are not stopped
- AI transformation loses executive confidence over time
- Business domains resist AI adoption without demonstrated value

---

## ROI Measurement Categories

### 1. Efficiency Gains
Reduction in time, cost, or resource consumption.

Examples:
- Engineering hours saved through AI-assisted code review
- Reduction in requirements defects detected late in the V-cycle
- Faster test cycle completion through AI-driven test prioritization

Measurement: Before/after comparison with control groups where feasible.

### 2. Quality Improvements
Reduction in defects, rework, or failures.

Examples:
- Reduction in field warranty claims from predictive diagnostics
- Decrease in safety-critical defect escape rate
- Improvement in software integration test pass rate

Measurement: Defect rate trends, warranty cost tracking, customer quality metrics.

### 3. Revenue Impact
New revenue enabled or accelerated by AI.

Examples:
- Time-to-market acceleration for new vehicle features
- New connected services revenue enabled by vehicle AI
- Competitive win rate improvement from differentiated AI capabilities

Measurement: Revenue attribution modeling, competitive analysis.

### 4. Risk Reduction
Reduction in compliance, safety, or operational risk.

Examples:
- Improved SOTIF compliance through AI-assisted hazard analysis
- Reduced regulatory audit findings through AI governance tooling
- Decreased supply chain disruption through AI demand forecasting

Measurement: Risk register change, audit finding trends, incident rates.

---

## Measurement Framework

### Input Metrics (Track investment)
- AI headcount and cost
- Platform and infrastructure spend
- Training and enablement investment

### Output Metrics (Track delivery)
- Number of AI initiatives deployed
- Models in production
- Users actively using AI tools

### Outcome Metrics (Track business impact)
- Business KPIs directly influenced by AI (linked at initiative level)
- ROI per initiative
- Cumulative program ROI

---

## Reporting Cadence

| Audience | Frequency | Content |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| AI Steering Committee | Quarterly | Program ROI, portfolio status, risks |
| CTO | Monthly | Key initiative milestones, emerging opportunities |
| Domain Leads | Monthly | Domain-specific ROI metrics, resource utilization |
| Board / CEO | Annually | Strategic AI value summary, competitive positioning |

---

## Automotive-Specific Value Drivers

- **Warranty cost reduction** — Predictive diagnostics ROI is measurable in warranty claims avoided
- **Cycle time compression** — AI in requirements and testing directly impacts program timing
- **Regulatory compliance** — EU AI Act readiness has a risk-avoidance value that should be quantified
- **SDV competitive positioning** — Time-to-feature has direct market share implications
