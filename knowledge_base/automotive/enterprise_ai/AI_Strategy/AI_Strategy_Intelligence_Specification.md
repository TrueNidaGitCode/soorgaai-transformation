# AI Strategy — Intelligence Specification

**Domain:** Enterprise AI Transformation  
**Status:** Available  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## Purpose

This document is the master intelligence specification for the AI Strategy domain in SoorgaAI.
It defines the scope, structure, and retrieval logic for the AI Strategy agent.

The AI Strategy domain helps CTOs and senior technology leaders define, govern, and execute
a coherent enterprise AI strategy aligned with business objectives.

---

## Intelligence Scope

The AI Strategy intelligence covers:

- Enterprise AI vision and mission definition
- AI investment prioritization frameworks
- Organizational alignment for AI transformation
- AI governance and ethics foundation
- AI Center of Excellence design
- ROI measurement and business case development
- Strategic roadmap construction

---

## Key Questions the AI Strategy Agent Answers

1. How should an automotive enterprise define its AI transformation vision?
2. Which AI initiatives should be prioritized given business objectives and maturity?
3. How should AI strategy be governed at the enterprise level?
4. What organizational structure supports sustainable AI transformation?
5. How should AI ROI be measured and communicated to executives?
6. What ethical principles should govern the enterprise AI program?
7. How does AI strategy align with the overall corporate strategy?

## Automotive Industry Context

AI Strategy in the automotive industry must account for:

- Software-Defined Vehicle (SDV) transformation
- Regulatory requirements (ISO 26262, SOTIF, EU AI Act)
- Multi-domain engineering complexity (ADAS, Diagnostics, Connectivity, etc.)
- Long product development cycles
- Supply chain AI integration
- OEM and Tier-1 supplier dynamics
