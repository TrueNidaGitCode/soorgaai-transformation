# Purpose

Within the Artificial Intelligence industry, an AI Operating Model defines how AI strategy is executed through its organizational structure, governance, and delivery mechanisms. It focuses specifically on how teams are structured, how delivery and governance are coordinated, and how AI capabilities are embedded into daily operations to deliver strategic business outcomes.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Business Context

This capability is relevant to: 1. AI-first enterprises—organizations where AI is deeply embedded across value streams and decision-making, often featuring roles like Chief AI Officer and dynamic work structures. 2. Traditional enterprises undergoing AI transformation—established businesses adapting their legacy operating models to scale AI across functions through centers of excellence, federated teams, or hybrid models.

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Business Challenges

- Rigid legacy IT governance
- Decentralized AI initiatives fragment
- Lack of hybrid human‑AI team design
- Static funding cycles don’t align
- Coordination costs high across domains

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Workflows

- Strategic AI vision → CAIO alignment → Cross‑functional AI delivery team
- Business need → CoE prioritizes → Federated delivery → Governance feedback
- Model prototype → Hybrid human + AI workflow design → Deployment → Continuous orchestration
- Pilot → Operating model redesign → Scaling across functions → Outcome measurement

---

# Typical AI Opportunities

- Establishing a central AI CoE to advise and govern delivery
- Designing hybrid human‑AI team structures with defined roles
- Transitioning funding models to continuous outcome‑based cycles
- Moving from org charts to fluid work charts/networked teaming
- Embedding AI governance into real‑time decision orchestration

---

# Artificial Intelligence Principles

- Structure teams around outcomes, not static functions
- Ensure continuous coordination rather than periodic control
- Embed hybrid human‑AI workflows from design forward
- Align funding and governance to business metrics and agility
- Use dynamic teaming and fluid roles where AI is core

---

# Leadership Question

AI‑first enterprises: “How can we restructure around dynamic, horizontal AI‑enabled workstreams rather than fixed functions?”; Traditional enterprises: “How can we establish a governance‑backed AI operating model (e.g., CoE or hub‑and‑spoke) that scales delivery across business units aligned to outcomes?”
