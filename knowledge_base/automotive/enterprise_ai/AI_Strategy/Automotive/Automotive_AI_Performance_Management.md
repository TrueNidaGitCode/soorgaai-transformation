## Purpose

This document applies the Core AI Performance Management framework to the measurement context of automotive engineering and business operations.

> For the universal performance management framework, refer to: `Core/AI_Performance_Management.md`

---

## Automotive Measurement Context

Automotive AI performance measurement must account for characteristics that distinguish automotive from other industries:

- Long product cycles — AI investment value often materializes across multiple program generations
- Safety criticality — performance of AI in safety-adjacent domains requires additional measurement rigor
- Regulatory accountability — EU AI Act and ISO 26262 require documented performance evidence
- Engineering culture — metrics must be credible to both software and traditional automotive engineers

---

## Automotive Performance Dimensions

Applying the Core performance dimensions to automotive contexts:

### Efficiency

| AI Initiative | Efficiency Metric | Automotive Target |
|--------------|------------------|------------------|
| AI-assisted requirements generation | Requirements cycle time reduction | 30–50% reduction |
| AI test case generation | Test design time reduction | 40–60% reduction |
| AI code review | Review cycle time reduction | 20–40% reduction |
| Predictive maintenance (manufacturing) | Unplanned downtime reduction | 15–30% reduction |

---

### Quality

| AI Initiative | Quality Metric | Automotive Target |
|--------------|---------------|------------------|
| AI-assisted requirements | Requirements defect escape rate | Reduction in late-cycle defect discovery |
| Predictive diagnostics | Warranty claims avoided | Measurable reduction in field failure costs |
| Manufacturing visual inspection | Defect escape rate | Reduction in end-of-line failures |
| AI regression analysis | Regression detection time | Earlier detection within CI/CD pipeline |

---

### Strategic Value

Strategic value metrics are essential for sustaining long-term AI investment in automotive organizations.

| Dimension | Automotive Measure |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Feature delivery acceleration | Weeks/months reduction in program milestone achievement |
| ADAS validation coverage | Increase in scenario coverage achievable within budget |
| SDV platform AI capability | AI capability maturity rating of the vehicle platform |
| Competitive differentiation | Feature parity or leadership vs. benchmark competitors |

---

### Risk Reduction

| Risk Type | Automotive Metric |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Regulatory compliance | EU AI Act and ISO 26262 audit readiness score |
| Safety defect escape | Reduction in ASIL-rated defect escapes to system integration |
| Cyber risk | Reduction in ISO 21434 risk findings through AI-driven monitoring |
| Supply chain disruption | Reduction in unplanned supply interruptions through AI forecasting |

## Automotive Measurement Governance

Performance measurement in automotive must integrate with existing program governance:

- AI KPIs should be visible in program-level dashboards alongside traditional quality and timing metrics
- Safety-critical AI systems require documented performance evidence as part of the safety case
- EU AI Act high-risk AI systems require post-market performance monitoring

---

## Reporting in Automotive Organizations

| Audience | Frequency | Focus |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| CTO | Monthly | AI program portfolio, strategic value, emerging opportunities |
| Program Director | Per milestone gate | AI initiative status, risk, and delivery against plan |
| AI CoE Lead | Weekly | Execution metrics, adoption, and technical performance |
| Safety Engineering | Ongoing | Performance of AI in safety-adjacent or safety-critical functions |
| Board / CEO | Annually | Enterprise AI value, competitive position, regulatory posture |

---

## Key Takeaways

- Automotive AI performance measurement must span engineering efficiency, quality, strategic value, and regulatory risk.
- Long program cycles require sustained measurement — not just short-term ROI claims.
- Safety-critical AI demands documented performance evidence, not just business metrics.
- Regulatory performance (EU AI Act, ISO 26262) is a mandatory measurement dimension for automotive AI programs.
- AI performance metrics should appear alongside traditional automotive quality and timing metrics — not in isolated AI dashboards.
