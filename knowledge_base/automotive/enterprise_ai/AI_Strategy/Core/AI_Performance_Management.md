# AI Performance Management

## Purpose

AI Performance Management ensures that AI initiatives deliver measurable business value, maintain effective execution, and drive sustainable organizational transformation.

Successful AI transformations should measure not only financial outcomes but also the health of delivery teams and the organization's ability to adopt and scale AI capabilities.

---

# Core Principles

Successful AI transformations measure performance across three dimensions:

1. Value Creation
2. AI Delivery Health
3. Change Management

---

# 1. Value Creation

## Definition

Value Creation measures the business impact generated by AI initiatives.

AI investments should contribute to strategic objectives by improving customer experiences, operational efficiency, and business performance.

## Key Principles

* Focus on measurable business outcomes.
* Align AI initiatives with strategic goals.
* Track customer and operational improvements.
* Evaluate financial and competitive impact.

## Leadership Question

**Are our AI initiatives creating meaningful business value?**

---

# 2. AI Delivery Health

## Definition

AI Delivery Health measures the effectiveness of teams responsible for delivering AI solutions.

Healthy AI delivery teams consistently execute priorities, collaborate effectively, and deliver high-quality outcomes.

## Key Principles

* Monitor delivery effectiveness.
* Track progress against objectives.
* Encourage continuous improvement.
* Maintain execution quality.

## Leadership Question

**Are our AI teams effectively delivering business outcomes?**

---

# 3. Change Management

## Definition

Change Management measures the organization's ability to adopt and sustain AI-driven ways of working.

AI transformation succeeds when employees, leaders, and business functions embrace new capabilities and processes.

## Key Principles

* Build organizational capabilities.
* Support workforce adoption.
* Invest in AI skills development.
* Monitor transformation progress.

## Leadership Question

**Is our organization successfully adopting AI capabilities?**

---

# AI Performance Management Framework

```text
Value Creation
Measure business impact.

↓

AI Delivery Health
Measure execution effectiveness.

↓

Change Management
Measure organizational adoption.
```

# Automotive Context

For automotive organizations, AI performance management supports transformation across engineering, manufacturing, software development, quality, supply chain, and customer services.

Examples of measurable outcomes include:

* Faster product development.
* Improved software and vehicle quality.
* Increased manufacturing efficiency.
* Reduced operational costs.
* Better customer satisfaction.
* Enhanced workforce capabilities.

The objective is to ensure that AI investments create sustainable business value while strengthening organizational capabilities.

---

# Key Takeaways

* AI success should be measured through business outcomes.
* Effective AI delivery requires healthy execution teams.
* Organizational adoption is critical for long-term success.
* Performance management should balance value creation, execution, and change.
* AI transformation is successful when measurable business value and organizational capability improve together.
