# SoorgaAI AI Strategy Intelligence Specification

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The AI Strategy Intelligence Specification defines the knowledge architecture, intelligence services, retrieval architecture, and success criteria required for SoorgaAI to support AI strategy development and enterprise AI transformation.

The objective is to provide executive leaders, CTOs, and AI transformation teams with structured intelligence for planning, executing, governing, and measuring AI initiatives.

This specification serves as the authoritative reference for all AI Strategy capabilities within SoorgaAI.

---

# Mission

Enable organizations to build and execute effective AI strategies by combining industry best practices, business objectives, and practical implementation guidance.

The focus is on ensuring that AI initiatives create measurable business value while maintaining organizational trust and long-term sustainability.

---

# Scope

The AI Strategy domain covers the strategic aspects of enterprise AI transformation, including leadership, business alignment, organizational capabilities, performance management, and responsible AI practices.

The domain does not focus on specific AI technologies or implementation details but provides executive-level guidance for successful AI adoption.

---

# Knowledge Architecture

The AI Strategy domain consists of four core intelligence areas.

| Domain                      | Primary Objective                        |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| AI Initiative Leadership    | Lead AI transformation                   |
| Business Strategy Alignment | Connect AI with business objectives      |
| AI Operating Model          | Organize people, capabilities, and delivery teams for AI outcomes |
| AI ROI                      | Measure AI business value and investment returns                  |
