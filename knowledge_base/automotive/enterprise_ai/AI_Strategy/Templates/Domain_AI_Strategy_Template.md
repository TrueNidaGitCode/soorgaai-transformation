## Instructions for Domain Leaders

This template enables engineering and business domain leaders to translate the company AI strategy
into domain-specific execution plans.

Each domain (ADAS, Diagnostics, Validation, Manufacturing, etc.) should complete one instance of this template.

The domain AI strategy must align with the Company AI Strategy Template completed by your CTO.
It should not introduce priorities that contradict or duplicate enterprise-level decisions.
