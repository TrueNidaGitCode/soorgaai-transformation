# Purpose

Within the Artificial Intelligence industry and the domain of AI Use Cases, AI Use Case Classification organizes opportunities into Productivity, Functional, or Product AI. This capability enables structured identification, prioritization, and resource alignment for AI initiatives according to their strategic type.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Business Context

Organizations in the AI industry include consulting firms that help other businesses ideate and categorize AI use cases, AI platform providers offering decision frameworks or tooling for AI opportunity selection, and internal AI strategy teams at enterprises building classification methodologies for their own portfolios. Consulting and advisory firms, tool vendors, and internal strategy functions all benefit from clearly classifying AI use cases by type.

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Business Challenges

- Ambiguous classification criteria
- Overlap between productivity vs functional use
- Difficulty aligning to strategic goals
- Inconsistent terminology across stakeholders

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Workflows

- Brainstorm use cases → Map to productivity/functional/product → Prioritize by business value
- Collect candidate AI opportunities → Classify type → Score via impact/effort framework
- Survey teams’ AI ideas → Apply taxonomy definitions → Build classification guide
- Review opportunity list → Assign category → Feed into portfolio planning

---

# Common High-Effort Activities

- Manually debating classification definitions
- Reconciling classification across business units
- Aligning use-case type with strategic KPIs
- Training stakeholders on categories

---

# Typical AI Opportunities

- Enable tool vendor to embed categorization guidance into UI for users to label AI ideas
- Provide internal strategy team with templated classification rubric to streamline portfolio decisions
- Help consultants audit a company’s AI pipeline by tagging all ongoing use cases by type
- Support teams in mapping new ideas into taxonomy before impact/effort scoring

---

# Artificial Intelligence Principles

- Use clear, industry-aligned terminology: 'Productivity', 'Functional', 'Product' AI
- Surface classification definitions with examples so stakeholders align on what each type means
- Prioritize quick discernibility to aid impact/effort mapping workflows
- Allow for overlap with guidance on primary vs secondary type

---

# Leadership Question

“Which of our AI initiatives deliver direct productivity gains versus those enabling core functional capabilities versus those intended as standalone AI products — and how should we allocate resources accordingly?”
