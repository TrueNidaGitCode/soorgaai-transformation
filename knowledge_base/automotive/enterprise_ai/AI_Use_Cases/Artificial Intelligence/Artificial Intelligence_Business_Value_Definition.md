# Purpose

Within the Artificial Intelligence industry, the capability of Business Value Definition means precisely articulating and quantifying the expected business outcomes for each AI use case—such as cost savings, productivity gains, customer satisfaction improvement or revenue acceleration. This ensures that every proposed AI initiative can be evaluated against measurable, outcome-oriented metrics and aligned with strategic business objectives.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Business Context

In the AI industry, this capability is essential for: Standard adopters: organizations deploying AI use cases internally or for clients (e.g., enterprises building automation, AI-enhanced workflows) AI product firms: companies offering AI-powered products or platforms to clients, where each use case must demonstrate customer ROI

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Business Challenges

- Unclear baseline metrics
- Long ROI realization horizon
- Difficulty tracking indirect value
- Hype-driven misalignment
- Portfolio-level undervaluation

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Workflows

- Identify business goal → Define KPI baseline → Quantify target uplift → Validate post‑deployment
- Gather stakeholder expectations → Map success metrics (e.g., cost, revenue, experience) → Align use case → Track outcomes
- Pilot use case → Measure productivity delta → Compare against cost → Decide scale or stop

---

# Typical AI Opportunities

- Baseline automation benefits (e.g., handling time reduction)
- Quantify operational efficiency gain (cost per transaction)
- Measure customer satisfaction lift (CSAT, retention)
- Translate productivity improvements into revenue growth
- Incorporate AI cost tracking into FinOps

---

# Artificial Intelligence Principles

- Always start with a clear business metric—not the AI technology
- Define baseline and target in concrete numbers (e.g., reduce support time by 30%)
- Include both direct financial and indirect or behavioral outcomes
- Use portfolio view: compare use cases by value/time‑to‑value
- Ensure C‑suite (CIO, CFO) alignment on value and governance

---

# Leadership Question

Standard adopters: "Which internal AI use cases will deliver tangible cost, productivity or customer satisfaction gains within 12–24 months?" AI product firms: "Which product features powered by AI can we quantify in customer ROI terms—such as efficiency, revenue uplift or retention—and use to drive adoption?"
