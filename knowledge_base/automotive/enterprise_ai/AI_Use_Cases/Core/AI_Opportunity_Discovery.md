# AI Opportunity Discovery

## Purpose

AI Opportunity Discovery is the first stage of the AI use case lifecycle. Its purpose is to identify business problems where AI can create measurable value by improving productivity, enhancing decision-making, or optimizing business processes.

Successful AI initiatives begin with business needs rather than technology. By understanding organizational objectives, operational challenges, and day-to-day workflows, project and product managers can identify opportunities where AI delivers meaningful and sustainable business outcomes.

---

# 1. AI Opportunity Discovery

## Definition

AI Opportunity Discovery is the structured process of identifying business problems that are suitable for AI. It begins by understanding the business challenge, analysing the current workflow, and recognising activities where AI can improve productivity, support decision-making, enhance quality, or accelerate business outcomes.

Strong AI opportunities typically emerge from recurring, knowledge-intensive, or time-consuming activities that have a measurable impact on business performance. The outcome of this capability is a well-defined AI opportunity statement that provides a clear foundation for classification, business value assessment, and prioritisation.

### Discovery Framework

AI Opportunity Discovery follows three simple steps:

**Understand the Business Problem**
* Identify the business objective or operational challenge.
* Understand who is affected and why it matters.
* Define the desired business outcome.

**Analyse the Current Workflow**
* Identify the major activities within the current process.
* Highlight activities that consume significant time, require expert knowledge, or involve repetitive manual effort.
* Understand where delays, inconsistencies, or quality issues occur.

**Recognise AI Opportunities**
* Look for activities involving repetitive analysis, knowledge retrieval, decision support, content generation, prediction, or classification.
* Describe the opportunity in business terms, focusing on the expected improvement rather than the technology.

## Key Principles

* Start with business problems before considering AI solutions.
* Focus on improving business outcomes rather than adopting AI for its own sake.
* Identify recurring activities with measurable business impact.
* Prioritise opportunities where AI augments human expertise and decision-making.
* Express AI opportunities using clear business language that stakeholders understand.

## Leadership Question

**Which recurring business activities consume significant effort, rely on human expertise, or delay business outcomes, and how could AI improve them?**
