# Business Value Definition

## Purpose

Business Value Definition is the third capability in the AI Transformation Journey.

Building upon the AI opportunities identified and classified in the previous capabilities, this capability defines the measurable business value expected from EACH opportunity before implementation begins.

Rather than evaluating AI technology, it establishes why each opportunity is worth investing in, who benefits, and how success will be measured. The outcome is a Business Value Map — one entry per identified opportunity — that guides prioritisation, investment decisions, and implementation planning.

---

# 1. Business Value Definition

## Definition

Business Value Definition is the structured process of identifying the measurable outcomes each identified AI opportunity is expected to deliver.

It translates each identified AI opportunity and its classification into tangible business improvements, ensuring that implementation decisions are driven by business impact rather than technology adoption.

The outcome of this capability is a value entry for every identified opportunity, each supported by measurable success metrics.

## Consultant Reasoning Process

For EACH identified AI opportunity, reason using the following sequence.

1. Review the opportunity and its classification from the previous capability.
2. Identify where its greatest business value will be created.
3. Determine the single value area it belongs to.
4. Recommend measurable success metrics for that value area.
5. Summarise the combined business impact across all opportunities in a single insight.

Repeat for every opportunity in the "Identified AI Opportunities" list — do not define value for only one.

## Business Value Framework

### Step 1 — Review Each AI Opportunity

For each opportunity, consider:
- Business objective it serves
- Its classification (Productivity AI, Functional AI, or Product AI)
- Expected organisational impact

### Step 2 — Identify the Business Value Area

Determine where each opportunity delivers its greatest measurable value.

**Engineering Productivity**

Use when the initiative primarily helps engineers complete activities more efficiently.

Typical outcomes:
- Reduced manual effort
- Faster engineering activities
- Increased engineering capacity

**Engineering Excellence**

Use when the initiative improves quality, consistency, or delivery processes.

Typical outcomes:
- Improved traceability
- Increased test coverage
- Reduced defects
- Standardised execution

**Project & Operational Performance**

Use when the initiative improves project execution, delivery performance, or operational decisions.

Typical outcomes:
- Faster issue resolution
- Improved delivery predictability
- Reduced rework
- Better resource utilisation

**Customer & Product Value**

Use when the initiative delivers measurable improvements to product quality or customer experience.

Typical outcomes:
- Improved customer satisfaction
- Higher product quality
- Competitive differentiation
- Revenue growth

### Step 3 — Define Success Measures

For every opportunity:
- Identify current baseline if known.
- Recommend measurable target outcomes.
- Define success metrics that can be tracked after implementation.

## Output Structure

Present the assessment using the following three sections. Do not include a Strategic Position field — this capability builds directly on the future-state already established by AI Opportunity Discovery earlier in the journey, so repeating it here is redundant.

### 1. Opportunity Value Map

For every opportunity in the "Identified AI Opportunities" list, in the same order, present:
- The opportunity name (copied verbatim — do not reword it)
- The single business value area it belongs to: Engineering Productivity, Engineering Excellence, Project & Operational Performance, or Customer & Product Value (whichever this opportunity's value is most concentrated in — multiple opportunities can share the same area if that's genuinely where their value lands)
- A one-line focus statement naming what this opportunity actually automates or changes (e.g. "Automate traceability mapping via semantic linking" — not "Improve engineering team efficiency")
- Two to three concise expected outcomes specific to this opportunity and company (3–6 words each)

Reject any focus statement or outcome that is a generic AI value claim with no mechanism (e.g. "Improves efficiency" or "Reduces errors" alone is not acceptable). A reader must be able to tell what this specific opportunity does, not just that it's beneficial.

### 2. Key Performance Indicators

Recommend exactly six measurable KPI names created by these opportunities collectively.

Each KPI should be a short metric label in title case (e.g. "Effort Reduction", "Cycle Time Improvement", "Quality Score").

Make every KPI specific to the company context — not generic engineering metrics.

### 3. Business Value Insight

Provide one to two sentences synthesising the combined business value across all identified opportunities — which one creates the most leverage and why, or how they compound together. Do not restate a single opportunity's value in isolation.

Reject a generic value claim untethered to how the opportunities work (e.g. "This will improve productivity across the team" is not acceptable without saying which activity it changes and how). If a data handling, security, or external-AI-service constraint was established during AI Opportunity Discovery, keep the value claim realistic for that constraint rather than implying unrestricted cloud-scale AI.

## Key Principles

- Define value for every identified opportunity — not just one.
- Focus on business outcomes rather than AI capabilities.
- Multiple opportunities may share a value area — assign each to wherever its value genuinely lands.
- Recommend meaningful, trackable success metrics.
- Keep outputs concise and executive-friendly.

## Leadership Question

**What measurable business outcomes will justify investing in each identified AI opportunity, and how will success be demonstrated?**

## Quality Checklist

Before completing this capability verify that:

- Every identified AI opportunity is given a value entry, not just one
- Each opportunity name is copied verbatim from the "Identified AI Opportunities" list
- Each opportunity has a value area, a focus statement, and 2-3 outcomes
- Exactly six KPI names are provided
- Business value insight synthesises across all opportunities, not just one
- Output is understandable within 30 seconds by a Project Manager

## Expected Outcome

At the completion of this capability the Project Manager should clearly understand:

- Why each identified AI opportunity creates business value
- Which value area each opportunity belongs to
- How success should be measured
- How the opportunities are prepared for prioritisation
