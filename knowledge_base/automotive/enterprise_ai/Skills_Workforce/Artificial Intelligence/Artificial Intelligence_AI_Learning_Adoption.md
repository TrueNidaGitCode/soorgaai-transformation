# Purpose

Within the artificial intelligence industry focused on skills & workforce, "AI Learning & Adoption" means designing targeted programs that build both foundational AI literacy and role‑specific competencies for business professionals and technical practitioners. It extends the Core capability by embedding AI adoption strategies into workforce development, ensuring employees can both understand and effectively apply AI tools responsibly and productively.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Business Context

Companies in the AI industry pursuing AI Learning & Adoption typically fall into two distinct sub‑categories:
- Enterprise service providers and technology firms delivering AI solutions and internal transformation (e.g., Pluralsight, General Assembly, Lloyds Banking Group’s AI training initiative)
- Workforce development intermediaries and public sector initiatives supporting broad AI upskilling (e.g., NSF’s AI workforce development hubs, Department of Labor AI Literacy Framework, EDA’s AI Upskill Accelerator, NAWI infrastructure)

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Business Challenges

- low employee AI literacy levels
- training not scaled across workforce
- disconnect between adoption and competency
- lack of validated readiness signals
- fragmented learning pathways
- poor manager support for AI use

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Workflows

- Assess existing workflows needing AI support → Define role‑specific AI literacy → Design blended program (on‑demand, workshops, hands‑on) → Measure adoption and business outcomes
- Identify AI use cases with employers → Co‑design curriculum via EDA pilot → Deploy cohort training → Track skill application and productivity impact
- Set competency framework (DoL or NAWI) → Facilitate learning modules → Validate readiness via assessment → Map to career or apprenticeship pathways

---

# Typical AI Opportunities

- Develop structured AI Academies linking skills to business outcomes (e.g., Pluralsight AI Academy)
- Leverage policy frameworks for AI literacy (e.g., DoL AI Literacy Framework) to guide program design
- Implement readiness infrastructure like NAWI Compass to track skill validation and career pathways
- Create employer‑led upskill programs via pilots (e.g., EDA’s AI Upskill Accelerator)
- Embed manager training to foster adoption and support (informed by Gallup research showing manager support drives usage)

---

# Artificial Intelligence Principles

- Anchor learning in real job tasks and AI use cases, not abstract knowledge
- Use validated competency frameworks and readiness signals to measure impact
- Blend structured and social learning—formal training plus peer‑supported, experiential practice
- Enable manager and leader engagement to model adoption behaviors
- Design for scalable continuity—lifelong learning, upskill pathways, not one‑off events

---

# Leadership Question

Service providers / technology firms: “How will we link our AI learning offering to measurable business adoption and productivity gains?”; Workforce development intermediaries / public initiatives: “How can we design AI literacy programs with validated readiness signals and pathways into employment or apprenticeship?”
