# Purpose

In the Artificial Intelligence industry, AI Roles & Capability Planning ensures organizations accurately identify the specific roles, responsibilities, and technical competencies required to develop, operationalize, and govern AI solutions. This capability builds on the core objective—defining roles and AI capabilities—but tailors it to AI’s unique, rapidly evolving talent spectrum and governance demands.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Business Context

This capability is relevant to companies across several AI-focused business types: • AI product and platform developers (e.g. startups, cloud AI vendors) responsible for building foundational models, inference infrastructure, and tools. • AI service integrators and consultancies that design and deploy AI solutions for external clients, including government agencies. • In-house AI centers within non-tech enterprises (e.g. banks, healthcare systems) embedding AI into business processes, governance, or workforce augmentation.

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Business Challenges

- Blurred research‑engineering boundaries (AI developers)
- Scaling talent with governance skills (AI developers)
- Embedding AI literacy across roles (in‑house centers)
- Reskilling skilled workforce rapidly (in‑house centers)
- Role & skills architecture lag (service integrators)
- Clients’ demand unclear competency needs (service integrators)

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Workflows

- Define AI initiative goals → Map required capabilities → Identify roles and gaps → Recruit or upskill
- Survey current workforce skills → Run AI literacy modules → Redesign job descriptions → Embed AI responsibilities
- Work with client → Analyze required AI functions → Recommend team structure → Source talent or train

---

# Typical AI Opportunities

- Design AI Roles Continuum across research-engineering spectrum (AI developers)
- Embed AI governance and ethics roles into team architecture (AI developers)
- Use AI literacy as mandatory job descriptor category (in‑house centers)
- Implement staged AI skills planning maturity model (in‑house centers)
- Restructure role architecture to include AI agent oversight and composite skills (service integrators)
- Provide clients with modular role-skill maps for workforce planning (service integrators)

---

# Artificial Intelligence Principles

- Treat roles as fluid across research, engineering, applied functions, not fixed titles
- Mandate AI literacy baseline in all job descriptions, even if currently ‘none’
- Prioritize AI governance, ethics, and risk skills along with technical capabilities
- Use staged maturity framing (early, intermediate, advanced) to align planning efforts
- Emphasize composite human‑AI collaboration skills (judgment, leadership, oversight)
