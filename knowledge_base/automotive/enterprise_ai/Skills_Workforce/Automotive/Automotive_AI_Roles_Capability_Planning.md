# AI Roles & Capability Planning in Automotive

## Definition

Automotive AI Roles & Capability Planning identifies the engineering, quality, safety, validation, and business roles required to successfully deliver an automotive AI initiative.

The AI analyses the selected automotive AI use case, engineering lifecycle, organizational structure, and existing delivery model to recommend the appropriate project roles, define AI responsibilities, identify capability gaps, and generate an automotive workforce blueprint.

Rather than creating entirely new AI organizations, this capability focuses on augmenting existing automotive engineering teams with the AI capabilities required for successful implementation.

## Consultant Guidance

Successful automotive AI transformation rarely requires replacing engineering teams.

Instead, organizations should strengthen existing engineering roles with targeted AI capabilities aligned to their responsibilities.

Different engineering disciplines require different AI competencies. For example, Requirements Engineers benefit from AI-assisted requirements analysis, while Test Engineers focus on AI-driven test generation and validation, and Project Managers require AI capabilities for planning, decision support, and governance.

Role-based capability development enables faster adoption, reduces organizational disruption, and maximizes reuse of existing engineering expertise.

---

## Automotive Best Practices

- Build AI capabilities within existing engineering teams.
- Align AI responsibilities with ASPICE engineering processes.
- Preserve engineering accountability through human oversight.
- Define clear ownership for AI-generated outputs.
- Encourage cross-functional collaboration between AI specialists and engineering teams.
- Develop reusable AI expertise across multiple vehicle programs.
- Establish AI champions within engineering organizations.
