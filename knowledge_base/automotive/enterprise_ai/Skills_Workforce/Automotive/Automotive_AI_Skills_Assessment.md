# Automotive AI Skills Assessment

## Purpose

Provide automotive-specific guidance for identifying the engineering, product, and domain skills required to successfully implement AI solutions across the vehicle development lifecycle.

> For the universal framework, refer to: `Core/AI Skills Assessment.md`

---

## AI Skills Assessment in Automotive

### Business Skills

Identify business competencies required for automotive AI initiatives.

Typical examples:

- Product management
- Program management
- Customer requirements analysis
- Business value definition
- Automotive project planning

---

### AI & Data Skills

Assess AI competencies required for engineering AI solutions.

Typical examples:

- Prompt engineering
- Machine learning fundamentals
- Engineering data analysis
- AI model evaluation
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- AI-assisted engineering

---

### Engineering Skills

Identify engineering capabilities required for implementation.

Typical examples:

- Embedded software development
- System engineering
- Software architecture
- API integration
- Cloud engineering
- Test automation
- DevOps

---

### Automotive Domain Expertise

Assess automotive engineering knowledge required by the AI solution.

Typical examples:

- Requirements engineering
- Functional safety
- Diagnostics
- AUTOSAR
- Vehicle architecture
- Verification & Validation
- Calibration
- Cybersecurity

---

### Skill Gap Analysis

Evaluate current engineering capabilities and identify missing competencies.

Typical examples:

- Missing AI expertise
- Limited engineering knowledge
- Training requirements
- External specialist support

### Key Principles

- Balance AI expertise with automotive engineering knowledge.
- Prioritize critical engineering skills.
- Reuse existing engineering capabilities.
- Strengthen cross-functional collaboration.
- Continuously build AI competency within engineering teams.

### Leadership Question

**Does our engineering team have the skills required to successfully deliver this automotive AI solution?**

---

## Automotive Best Practices

- Combine AI specialists with experienced automotive engineers.
- Upskill engineering teams in AI-assisted development.
- Promote collaboration across software, systems, testing, and product teams.
- Focus training on practical engineering use cases.
- Encourage knowledge sharing across vehicle programs.
