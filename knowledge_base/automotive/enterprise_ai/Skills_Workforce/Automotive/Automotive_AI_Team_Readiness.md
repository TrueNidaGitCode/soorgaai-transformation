# Automotive AI Team Readiness

## Purpose

Provide automotive-specific guidance for assessing whether the engineering team has the appropriate roles and collaboration required to successfully implement AI solutions across the vehicle development lifecycle.

> For the universal framework, refer to: `Core/AI Team Readiness.md`

### Key Principles

- Build multidisciplinary engineering teams.
- Combine AI expertise with automotive domain knowledge.
- Define clear engineering ownership.
- Encourage collaboration across engineering disciplines.
- Address skill and role gaps before implementation.

### Leadership Question

**Does our engineering organization have the right team to successfully implement this automotive AI solution?**

---

## Automotive Best Practices

- Include engineering domain experts throughout the AI lifecycle.
- Combine software, systems, testing, and AI expertise.
- Clearly define ownership for engineering decisions.
- Encourage cross-functional collaboration.
- Validate AI recommendations using domain expertise.
