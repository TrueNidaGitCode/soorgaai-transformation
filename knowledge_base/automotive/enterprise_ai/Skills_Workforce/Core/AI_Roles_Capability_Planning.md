# AI Roles & Capability Planning

## Purpose

Identify the roles, responsibilities, and AI capabilities required to successfully deliver, operate, and continuously improve the selected AI use case.

Rather than recommending organization-wide AI training, this capability helps project managers determine which project roles require AI capabilities, what responsibilities they will assume, and where capability gaps exist before AI implementation begins.

---

# 1. AI Roles & Capability Planning

## Definition

AI Roles & Capability Planning is the process of identifying the people, roles, responsibilities, and AI capabilities required to successfully implement and sustain an AI solution.

The AI analyses the selected AI use case, delivery approach, technology landscape, and organizational context to recommend the appropriate project roles, define AI responsibilities, identify capability gaps, and generate a practical workforce plan.

The objective is not to transform every employee into an AI expert, but to ensure the right people possess the right AI capabilities at the right stage of the implementation.

---

## Framework

### Business & Delivery Roles

Identify the business and delivery roles responsible for defining, managing, and governing the AI initiative.

Typical examples:

- Executive Sponsor
- Product Owner
- Project Manager
- Business Analyst
- Scrum Master
- Delivery Manager

---

### AI Solution Roles

Identify specialist roles required to design, develop, deploy, and maintain the AI solution.

Typical examples:

- AI Solution Architect
- AI Engineer
- Data Engineer
- Machine Learning Engineer
- Prompt Engineer
- AI Platform Engineer
- MLOps Engineer

---

### Domain & Functional Roles

Identify subject matter experts who provide business context and validate AI outcomes.

Typical examples:

- Requirements Engineer
- Test Engineer
- Software Developer
- System Architect
- Quality Engineer
- Customer Support Engineer
- Compliance Specialist

---

### AI Responsibilities

Define how responsibilities are distributed across project roles.

Typical examples:

- AI use case definition
- Data ownership
- Prompt design
- Model validation
- Human approval
- AI monitoring
- Risk management
- Knowledge management

---

### Capability Assessment

Determine whether existing project roles possess the capabilities required for successful AI implementation.

Typical examples:

- AI literacy
- Data literacy
- Prompt engineering
- AI governance
- AI-assisted decision making
- AI solution operations

---

### Capability Gap Analysis

Identify capability gaps that could delay or reduce the success of the AI initiative.

Typical examples:

- Missing AI Architect
- Limited Prompt Engineering capability
- Lack of AI governance ownership
- Insufficient AI operational knowledge
- Limited AI adoption champions

---

## Consultant Guidance

Successful AI transformation is driven by targeted capability development rather than organization-wide AI training.

Different roles require different AI capabilities.

Project managers should first identify the minimum set of roles required for the selected AI use case, define clear responsibilities, and assess existing capabilities before investing in training or recruiting additional specialists.

---

## Key Principles

- Identify only the roles required for the specific AI initiative already selected in AI Opportunity Discovery — do not plan for a different or generic AI use case.
- Assign clear AI responsibilities before implementation begins.
- Develop AI capabilities based on role responsibilities rather than job titles.
- Reuse existing project roles wherever practical.
- Build multidisciplinary AI delivery teams.
- Focus on capability development before organizational expansion.

---

## Leadership Question

**Do we have the right people, responsibilities, and AI capabilities required to successfully deliver and sustain this AI initiative?**

# AI Reasoning Process

```text
Understand AI Use Case
        ↓
Identify Required Roles
        ↓
Define AI Responsibilities
        ↓
Assess Existing Capabilities
        ↓
Identify Capability Gaps
        ↓
Generate AI Workforce Blueprint
```

# Blueprint Output

For every AI use case, the AI generates:

- Required Project Roles
- AI Responsibility Matrix
- Capability Assessment
- Capability Gap Analysis
- Recommended Role Allocation
- Workforce Development Priorities
- AI Workforce Blueprint
- AI Recommendations

Reject an AI capability that would fit any AI project role (e.g. "AI tools knowledge" alone is not acceptable). Name the specific technique from the selected initiative this role must understand or operate.

---

# Expected Outcome

After completing this capability, the project team should have:

- A clear understanding of the roles required to deliver the AI initiative.
- Defined AI responsibilities for each project role.
- Identified capability gaps that require development or recruitment.
- A practical workforce plan aligned with the selected AI use case.
- A strong foundation for role-based AI skills development in the next capability.

---

# AI Prompt Guidance

When generating the blueprint, the AI should:

- Identify the minimum set of roles required for the selected AI use case.
- Recommend responsibilities for each role throughout the AI lifecycle.
- Assess whether existing project teams possess the required capabilities.
- Highlight capability gaps and workforce risks.
- Recommend practical approaches to build or acquire missing capabilities.
- Focus on enabling successful AI delivery rather than maximizing team size.
