# AI Skills Assessment

## Purpose

Identify the business, technical, engineering, and domain skills required to successfully implement the selected AI use case.

Rather than assessing every possible competency, this capability helps project managers identify the critical skills needed for implementation, evaluate existing team capabilities, and prioritize skill gaps that may impact project success.

---

# 1. AI Skills Assessment

## Definition

AI Skills Assessment is the process of identifying the essential skills required for implementing an AI solution and evaluating whether the project team possesses those capabilities.

The AI analyzes the selected AI use case, recommends the required competencies, identifies missing skills, and prioritizes areas that require additional expertise or training.

## Framework

### Business Skills

Identify the business knowledge required to define, validate, and measure AI outcomes.

Typical examples: Business process understanding, stakeholder management, business analysis, value realization, KPI definition.

---

### AI & Data Skills

Assess the AI and data competencies required to develop the solution.

Typical examples: AI fundamentals, prompt engineering, machine learning concepts, data analysis, data preparation, AI model evaluation.

---

### Engineering Skills

Identify the engineering capabilities required to build and integrate the AI solution.

Typical examples: Software development, API integration, cloud platforms, DevOps, system integration, testing.

---

### Domain Expertise

Assess the domain-specific knowledge required to ensure accurate AI recommendations.

Typical examples: Product knowledge, regulatory understanding, operational workflows, customer processes, industry standards.

---

### Skill Gap Analysis

Compare required skills with existing team capabilities to identify implementation risks.

Typical examples: Missing competencies, training needs, external expertise, coaching opportunities.

## Key Principles

- Identify only the skills required for the selected AI use case.
- Balance business, technical, engineering, and domain expertise.
- Focus on closing critical skill gaps before implementation.
- Leverage existing team capabilities whenever possible.
- Treat skills as an enabler of successful AI implementation.

## Leadership Question

**Does our project team have the skills required to successfully implement this AI use case?**

# AI Reasoning Process

```text
Understand AI Use Case
        ↓
Identify Required Skills
        ↓
Classify Skills
        ↓
Assess Existing Capabilities
        ↓
Identify Skill Gaps
        ↓
Generate Skill Recommendations
```

---

# Blueprint Output

For every AI use case, the AI generates a project-specific skills blueprint covering:

- Required Business Skills
- Required AI & Data Skills
- Required Engineering Skills
- Required Domain Expertise
- Skill Gap Analysis
- Priority Learning Areas
- Recommended Actions

---

# Expected Outcome

After completing this capability, the project team should understand the critical skills required for the selected AI use case, identify existing capability gaps, and receive prioritized recommendations to prepare the team for successful AI implementation.
