# AI Team Readiness

## Purpose

Assess whether the project team has the appropriate roles, responsibilities, and collaboration required to successfully implement the selected AI use case.

This capability helps project managers identify missing roles, clarify responsibilities, and ensure the right expertise is available throughout the AI implementation lifecycle.

---

# 1. AI Team Readiness

## Definition

AI Team Readiness is the process of evaluating whether the project team includes the necessary business, technical, engineering, and domain expertise required to deliver an AI solution successfully.

The AI analyzes the selected AI use case, recommends key project roles, identifies responsibility gaps, and highlights areas where additional expertise may be required.

## Framework

### Business Leadership

Identify the business roles responsible for defining objectives and measuring business value.

Typical examples:

Product Owner, Product Manager, Business Analyst, Project Manager.

---

### AI Specialists

Identify AI-specific expertise required for implementation.

Typical examples:

AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, Data Scientist, Prompt Engineer.

---

### Engineering Team

Identify engineering roles responsible for building and integrating the solution.

Typical examples:

Software Engineer, System Architect, API Developer, DevOps Engineer, Test Engineer.

---

### Domain Experts

Identify subject matter experts required to validate AI recommendations.

Typical examples:

Operations Expert, Product Expert, Customer Support Expert, Regulatory Expert.

---

### Responsibility Coverage

Evaluate whether responsibilities are clearly assigned across the project lifecycle.

Typical examples:

Decision ownership, technical ownership, validation ownership, operational ownership.

## Key Principles

- Build cross-functional AI teams.
- Clearly define ownership and responsibilities.
- Balance business and technical expertise.
- Include domain experts early.
- Identify missing roles before implementation.

## Leadership Question

**Do we have the right project team to successfully deliver this AI solution?**

---

# AI Reasoning Process

```text
Understand AI Use Case
        ↓
Identify Required Roles
        ↓
Evaluate Existing Team
        ↓
Identify Missing Roles
        ↓
Assess Responsibility Coverage
        ↓
Generate Team Recommendations
```

---

# Blueprint Output

For every AI use case, the AI generates:

- Recommended Project Team
- Required Roles
- Missing Roles
- Responsibility Assessment
- Collaboration Recommendations
- Team Readiness Score

---

# Expected Outcome

After completing this capability, the project team should understand whether the right people are available to implement the selected AI use case and receive recommendations to strengthen team readiness.
