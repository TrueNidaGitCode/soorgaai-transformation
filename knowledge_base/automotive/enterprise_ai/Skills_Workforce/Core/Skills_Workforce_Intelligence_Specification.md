# SoorgaAI Skills & Workforce Intelligence Specification

Version 1.0

---

# Purpose

The Skills & Workforce Intelligence Specification defines the knowledge architecture and intelligence services required for SoorgaAI to assess and develop the human capabilities needed to deliver, sustain, and scale AI initiatives within enterprise programs and delivery teams.

The objective is to provide project managers, product managers, and delivery leads with a structured pipeline for evaluating current AI skills, assessing team readiness, and designing targeted learning and adoption strategies that build lasting AI competency across the organisation.

This specification serves as the authoritative reference for all Skills & Workforce capabilities within SoorgaAI.

---

# Mission

Enable delivery teams to build the human foundation required for sustained AI success by identifying skill gaps, structuring teams for AI delivery, and designing learning programs that translate AI knowledge into practical capability.

---

# Scope

The Skills & Workforce domain covers the end-to-end process of assessing whether an organisation's people, teams, and learning programs can support AI implementation — from identifying individual skill gaps through to driving organisation-wide AI adoption.

The domain does not cover technology selection, data preparation, or AI strategy formulation — those belong to separate domains.

---

# Knowledge Architecture

The Skills & Workforce domain consists of three core capabilities that work together as an assessment and development pipeline.

| Domain                  | Primary Objective                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI Roles & Capability Planning | Identify the roles, responsibilities, and AI capabilities required to deliver the initiative |
| AI Team Readiness       | Assess whether team structure and resourcing are set up to deliver AI effectively   |
| AI Learning & Adoption  | Design targeted learning programs and adoption strategies to build AI competency    |

---

# Pipeline Overview

A user identifies an AI use case. SoorgaAI processes it through the three capabilities in sequence.

Example input:

> "We want to deploy an AI defect prediction model but are unsure whether our team has the skills to build and run it."

Processing:

1. **AI Roles & Capability Planning** — Identifies the project roles, AI responsibilities, and capability gaps required for the AI use case. Evaluates whether existing team members can fulfil each role and flags missing capabilities before implementation begins.
2. **AI Team Readiness** — Assesses whether the team is structured correctly with clear AI roles, sufficient capacity, and the right collaboration model to deliver the initiative.
3. **AI Learning & Adoption** — Designs a targeted learning pathway to close identified skill gaps, selects appropriate learning formats, and builds an adoption strategy to embed AI practices into the team's daily workflow.

---

# Intelligence Services

## AI Roles & Capability Planning

Identify the project roles, AI responsibilities, and capability gaps required to successfully deliver, operate, and sustain the selected AI use case. Determine whether existing team members can fulfil each role and recommend capability development before implementation begins.

Key question:

**Do we have the right roles, responsibilities, and AI capabilities required to deliver this initiative?**

---

## AI Team Readiness

Assess whether the team's composition, roles, responsibilities, capacity, and ways of working are aligned to successfully deliver, operate, and iterate on AI solutions.

Key question:

**Is our team structured and resourced to deliver AI effectively?**

---

## AI Learning & Adoption

Design and implement targeted learning programs and adoption strategies that build practical AI competency across the team and drive sustained behaviour change throughout the organisation.

Key question:

**How do we build the AI capabilities our team needs?**

# Automotive Context

For automotive software organisations, skills and workforce challenges most frequently arise from:

* Bridging the gap between traditional embedded software engineers and AI/ML practitioners
* Building AI literacy among functional safety, systems, and validation engineers
* Upskilling engineering leads to evaluate and govern AI solutions
* Managing the transition from manual verification processes to AI-augmented workflows
* Attracting and retaining AI talent in a competitive engineering labour market
* Scaling AI skills across multi-site and supplier organisations

The objective is to help automotive delivery teams build the human capability required to deliver AI initiatives effectively, safely, and at scale.

# Success Criteria

The Skills & Workforce domain should enable delivery teams to:

* Identify the AI skills required for each initiative and assess current team capability.
* Evaluate whether team structure and resourcing are aligned to AI delivery.
* Design and execute targeted learning programs that close priority skill gaps.

# Key Takeaways

* Skills assessment must be anchored to the specific AI use case requirements — not generic AI frameworks.
* Team readiness depends on structure and clarity of roles as much as individual skill levels.
* Learning programs only deliver value when combined with a practical adoption strategy that embeds new skills into day-to-day work.
