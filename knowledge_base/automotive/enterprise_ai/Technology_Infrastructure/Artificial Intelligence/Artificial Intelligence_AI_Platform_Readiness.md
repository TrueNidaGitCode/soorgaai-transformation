# Purpose

Within the Artificial Intelligence industry’s Technology Infrastructure domain, AI Platform Readiness evaluates whether the organization’s compute, storage, network, governance, and data architecture are appropriately configured to support AI workloads in production rather than just experimentation. It builds on the Core definition by focusing on scaling agentic AI systems, addressing constraints like inference tax, power, hybrid topology, and governance embedded in infrastructure design.

---

# Artificial Intelligence Business Context

Relevant companies include: Hyperscalers and Cloud Providers (e.g., AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft) that offer foundational AI platforms as a service; Enterprise Technology Organizations that operate hybrid or on-prem AI infrastructure to support internal or customer-facing AI services.

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Business Challenges

- Inference tax from legacy architecture
- Compute scarcity for agentic workloads
- Power and energy constraints at scale
- Fragmented hybrid environments
- Governance/security integration challenges

---

# Typical Artificial Intelligence Workflows

- Pilot AI model → Evaluate infrastructure bottlenecks → Upgrade compute/network → Deploy to production
- Define AI use case → Assess inference tax and operational complexity → Architect fluid compute topology → Operate agentic AI
- Site selection → Validate power and grid readiness → Provision infrastructure → Scale AI deployment

---

# Common High-Effort Activities

- Benchmarking inference costs on legacy systems
- Evaluating hybrid multicloud network performance
- Modeling power and cooling needs for data center AI racks
- Integrating governance and security for agent orchestration

---

# Typical AI Opportunities

- Implement fluid compute to dynamically match task to silicon
- Adopt hybrid multicloud architecture to reduce latency and increase resilience
- Design energy-aware infrastructure and site selection strategy
- Embed governance and MLOps in platform control plane

---

# Artificial Intelligence Principles

- Use terminology rooted in agentic AI and inference tax rather than generic AI readiness
- Prioritize dynamic compute flexibility and energy efficiency
- Differentiate between cloud-native hyperscalers vs. enterprise hybrid contexts
- Embed security and governance as part of infrastructure, not add-on

---

# Leadership Question

Hyperscalers/Cloud Providers: "How are we designing platform capabilities so fluid compute, governance, and cost control are intrinsic to scaling agentic AI?" Enterprise Technology Organizations: "Do our hybrid multicloud infrastructure, power planning, and control-plane architecture truly support production-grade autonomous AI workloads?"
