# AI Engineering Enablement

## Purpose

Assess whether the engineering environment, development practices, automation, and collaboration capabilities are sufficient to successfully develop, deploy, and maintain the selected AI solution.

This capability helps project managers identify engineering bottlenecks and recommends improvements that accelerate AI delivery while maintaining quality and reliability.

---

# 1. AI Engineering Enablement

## Definition

AI Engineering Enablement is the process of evaluating whether engineering teams have the tools, workflows, automation, and operational practices required to efficiently build and maintain AI solutions.

The AI assesses development workflows, testing, deployment automation, collaboration, and operational maturity to identify opportunities for improving engineering productivity.

## Framework

### Development Environment

Assess whether engineering teams have an efficient development environment.

Typical examples:

Integrated development tools, AI-assisted development, collaborative workspaces, reusable engineering assets.

---

### Engineering Workflow

Evaluate how AI development activities are planned and executed.

Typical examples:

Agile workflows, backlog management, sprint planning, code reviews, engineering collaboration.

---

### Testing & Validation

Assess how AI solutions are verified before deployment.

Typical examples:

Automated testing, model evaluation, regression testing, validation workflows, quality assurance.

---

### Deployment Automation

Evaluate deployment automation across the AI lifecycle.

Typical examples:

CI/CD pipelines, automated deployment, release management, infrastructure automation.

---

### Monitoring & Continuous Improvement

Assess operational feedback and continuous improvement practices.

Typical examples:

Operational monitoring, incident management, user feedback, continuous optimization.

---

### Team Collaboration

Evaluate how engineering teams collaborate while building AI solutions.

Typical examples:

Knowledge sharing, documentation, reusable assets, cross-functional collaboration.

## Key Principles

- Automate repetitive engineering activities.
- Standardize AI engineering practices.
- Encourage collaboration and reuse.
- Continuously improve engineering productivity.
- Deliver production-ready AI solutions efficiently.

## Leadership Question

**Can our engineering teams efficiently build, test, deploy, and continuously improve this AI solution?**

---

# Decision Criteria

The AI evaluates:
