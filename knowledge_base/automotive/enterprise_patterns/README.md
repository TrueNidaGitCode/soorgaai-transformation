# Enterprise Patterns

Reusable enterprise AI concepts applicable across multiple business domains and transformation programs.

## Purpose

Enterprise patterns capture proven organizational and operational approaches that recur
across AI transformation programs. Storing them centrally avoids duplication and ensures
consistent guidance across domains.

## Pattern Library

| Pattern | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| AI_CoE.md | AI Center of Excellence structure and operating models |
| AI_Governance.md | Governance frameworks for enterprise AI programs |
| AI_ROI.md | ROI measurement and business value communication |
| AI_Operating_Model.md | Operating model design for AI-transformed organizations |
| Change_Management.md | Managing organizational change through AI transformation |

## Usage by AI Agents

When generating domain-specific guidance, retrieve relevant patterns and combine with
domain intelligence to produce contextualized, non-generic recommendations.

Pattern retrieval should come AFTER enterprise AI domain context and BEFORE shared reference information.
