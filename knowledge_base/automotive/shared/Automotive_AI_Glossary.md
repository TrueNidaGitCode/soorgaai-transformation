# Automotive AI Glossary

Common terms and definitions used across the SoorgaAI Automotive Knowledge Base.

---

## Enterprise AI Terms

**AI Center of Excellence (AI CoE)**  
A centralized organizational function that defines standards, builds shared platforms,
and supports business domains in AI transformation.

**AI Maturity**  
The level of organizational readiness and capability to deliver, govern, and scale AI.
SoorgaAI measures maturity across five stages.

**AI Operating Model**  
The organizational, process, and technology structure through which AI transformation
is delivered and sustained.

**AI Transformation**  
The systematic process of embedding AI across an organization's strategy, operations,
and engineering to create sustainable competitive advantage.

**Enterprise AI Strategy**  
A structured plan defining AI vision, investment priorities, governance, and roadmap
at the enterprise level, spanning all business and engineering domains.

**Hub-and-Spoke**  
An organizational model where a central AI CoE (hub) provides shared capabilities and
standards, and domain-embedded teams (spokes) execute domain-specific AI initiatives.

**MLOps**  
Machine Learning Operations — the practices and tooling for deploying, monitoring, and
maintaining machine learning models in production.

## Automotive Engineering Terms

**ADAS**  
Advanced Driver Assistance Systems. AI-driven systems that assist or automate driving tasks
(lane keeping, emergency braking, adaptive cruise control, etc.).

**ASPICE**  
Automotive SPICE (Software Process Improvement and Capability Determination).
The process assessment model used in automotive software development.

**AUTOSAR**  
Automotive Open System Architecture. A standardized software architecture for automotive ECUs.

**CAN Bus**  
Controller Area Network. The primary vehicle communication bus connecting ECUs.

**DTC**  
Diagnostic Trouble Code. A standardized fault code stored by vehicle ECUs.

**ECU**  
Electronic Control Unit. An embedded computer controlling a specific vehicle system.

**HARA**  
Hazard Analysis and Risk Assessment. A safety engineering process required by ISO 26262.

**ISO 26262**  
International standard for functional safety of road vehicles.

**OBD-II**  
On-Board Diagnostics version 2. The standard interface for vehicle diagnostic data.

**OTA**  
Over-The-Air. Software and firmware updates delivered wirelessly to vehicles.

**SDV**  
Software-Defined Vehicle. A vehicle architecture where functionality is primarily
defined and delivered through software rather than hardware.

**SOTIF**  
Safety of the Intended Functionality (ISO 21448). A standard addressing safety risks
from insufficiencies in the intended functionality of advanced driver assistance systems.

**V-Cycle**  
The automotive systems engineering development process model, showing development
and verification activities in a V-shaped diagram.
