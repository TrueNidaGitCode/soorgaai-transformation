# Best Practices — Enterprise AI in Automotive

Cross-domain best practices for enterprise AI transformation in automotive organizations.

---

## Strategy Best Practices

**Start with business problems, not AI technology**  
Define the business objective first. Then identify whether AI is the right solution.
Avoid deploying AI for its own sake.

**Align the first initiatives with executive priorities**  
First AI initiatives should visibly address a strategic priority. This builds executive
confidence and organizational permission for broader transformation.

**Define success metrics before you start**  
Establish the baseline and KPIs before deployment. ROI claims without a pre-defined
baseline are not credible.

**Build the platform while delivering value**  
Avoid a "platform first" approach that delays value delivery. Build infrastructure
incrementally alongside early use case delivery.

---

## Engineering Best Practices

**Human review gates for safety-adjacent AI outputs**  
AI-generated requirements, test cases, and architecture designs in safety-critical
domains must always pass through qualified human review. AI accelerates; engineers decide.

**Version control everything**  
Models, training data, prompts, and configurations should all be version-controlled.
Reproducibility is a governance requirement, not an optional practice.

**Monitor models in production**  
Deploy model performance monitoring from day one. Models degrade as data distributions shift.
Automotive engineering processes evolve; AI models trained on historical data must be kept current.

**Document AI involvement in engineering artefacts**  
For traceability and audit purposes, track which engineering artefacts were AI-assisted.
This is becoming a regulatory requirement under the EU AI Act.

---

## Organizational Best Practices

**Involve domain engineers in AI use case selection**  
Engineers who understand the problem space identify better use cases than AI teams
working in isolation. Co-creation increases adoption.

**Address workforce concerns proactively**  
Do not wait for resistance to emerge. Communicate the AI transformation vision,
the role of engineers in an AI-augmented workflow, and the plan for skills development.

**Build internal capability alongside vendor solutions**  
Dependency on a single vendor for core AI capability creates strategic risk.
Build sufficient internal expertise to evaluate, govern, and evolve AI solutions.

**Celebrate and share early wins**  
Create internal case studies from successful AI pilots. Engineer peer testimonials
are more persuasive than executive mandates for driving adoption.

---

## Governance Best Practices

**Classify risk before deploying**  
Every AI system should be classified by risk level before deployment.
Risk classification drives documentation, testing, and approval requirements.

**Establish the AI register early**  
Maintain a central registry of all AI systems in development and production.
This is foundational to governance and will be required for EU AI Act compliance.

**Integrate governance with existing processes**  
AI governance should augment, not replace, existing quality management,
safety engineering, and change management processes.

**Review governance annually**  
The regulatory and technology landscape is evolving rapidly. Governance frameworks
must be reviewed and updated at least annually.
